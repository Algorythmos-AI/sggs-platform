Hukamnama corrections log

19 November 2026 hukamnamaverification

This log lists every day the Hukamnama on Gurbani Soul was corrected after it was published, or held because it could not be proven. How the daily Hukamnama is verified explains what “held” means. A held day is the method working as intended: nothing unproven was shown in its place.

The public service began on 16 November 2026. Before that it ran only as a test.

19 November 2026 · held

The Hukamnama came from the Vaar of Raag Bilaval, Mahalla 4 (Ang 849): a salok of Guru Ram Das Ji, a salok of Guru Amar Das Ji and a pauri. SGPC’s page printed the labels for each salok and for the pauri among the verses. Our text stores those labels as heading lines, and the matching could not start on one, so the day did not resolve, although every word of the page matched our text. Nothing was published. The page went on showing 18 November, under its own date. The fix lets the matching start on a printed label. It is still word for word.

17 November 2026 · corrected

The Hukamnama (Ang 604, Raag Sorath, Guru Ram Das Ji) was first published without the two heading lines printed above it: the raag heading and the invocation, Ik Onkar. The verses themselves were complete and correct. The day was corrected 27 minutes after it was published, before the Hukamnama pages went live on this site. Since then, the heading printed above a shabad is always taken from our text.

Reporting an error

If you see something wrong, write to support. A corrected day keeps its first publication date, and its page says when it was corrected.
