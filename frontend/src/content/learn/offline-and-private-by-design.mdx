Gurbani Soul is built to be a quiet, private place to read. That is not a setting you turn on — it is
how the app is made.

## Everything is on your device

The complete Granth, the search index, Nitnem, the Hukam, the Raag Clock and the widgets all run from
data bundled inside the app. The app works in Airplane Mode, on a plane, in a Gurdwara basement, or
anywhere with no signal at all — because the Granth never needed a connection in the first place.

The one exception is the daily Hukamnama from Sri Harmandir Sahib. To show its card, page and widget,
the app asks our Hukamnama service which shabad is the day's Hukamnama. That request carries no token,
no identifier and nothing about you, and the text itself always comes from the copy of Sri Guru Granth
Sahib Ji inside the app.

## No account, no tracking

There is nothing to sign up for and no profile to create. The app keeps no analytics, embeds no
third-party tracking SDKs, and sends nothing about your reading anywhere. Your place in each bani,
the days you have read, and anything you save stay on your device.

## One item on the label

Apple asks every app to declare what it collects, on the App Store privacy label. Gurbani Soul's
label lists one item, **Device ID**: the notification token, collected only if you turn the Hukamnama
notification on, used only to deliver it, not linked to you and never used for tracking. Turning the
notification off deletes it. The app's bundled privacy manifest declares exactly that and nothing
else, so the claim on the listing and the behaviour of the app are the same thing.

## Why it matters for scripture

Reading Gurbani is a personal act, and it should not be watched, scored, or turned into a data trail.
Keeping everything on the device is also what lets the app verify the text offline: the scripture,
its checksum and its citations all travel together, and nothing you read, search for or save is ever
sent to a server.

You can read the full policy on the [privacy page](/en/privacy), and if you ever have a question or spot
something to correct, [support](/en/support) is a person, not a form that feeds a database. The same
verified text is also on the web in the [Knowledge Base](/search).
